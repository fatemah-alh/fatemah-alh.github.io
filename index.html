Fatemah Alhamdoosh

Computer Vision · Multimodal Machine Learning

I am a PhD researcher at the University of Florence’s MICC, working on computer vision, self-supervised learning and multimodal representations for human movement analysis and healthcare.

I developed EgoInertia-MI, an egocentric video–IMU benchmark for motor impairment assessment, and studied pain estimation from facial landmark dynamics.
